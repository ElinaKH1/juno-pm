## What it demonstrates

Juno can turn scattered product signals into a concise, evidence-based brief that helps a PM understand what matters, verify the supporting sources, and identify where PM judgment is needed without reopening every input.

_____

## Debrief

- **What worked:** The one-page structure made the main findings easy to scan, while the source references and previews kept the synthesis traceable.
- **What broke / felt like a toy:** The integrations and AI synthesis are simulated, so the prototype does not yet prove that Juno can retrieve and interpret real information reliably.
- **What I'd change next pass:** I would test the brief with PMs using more complex inputs and improve the layout based on what they find clear or difficult to identify.
