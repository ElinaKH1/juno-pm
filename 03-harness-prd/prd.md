## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need this handled: Juno turns scattered signals from customer feedback, Slack, Jira, meetings, sales notes, and product documents into a one-page, evidence-linked brief that highlights what matters, what needs clarification, and which risks or decisions require Product Manager attention.. Juno does this inside limits a human set on purpose, and defends every call it makes.

## 01 Context · Data Requirements

**Required sources, and exclusions**

Juno may use the current batch of raw signals, linked Jira tickets and Slack threads, the Product Strategy One-Pager, current Product Roadmap, Engineering Capacity Note, and relevant Sales Pipeline Snapshot entries. Strategy, roadmap, capacity, and sales documents provide decision context; they do not prove current user behavior. Juno must exclude unrelated records, inaccessible sources, outdated document versions, and any source not approved for the brief.

**How fresh must it be**

Juno retrieves the latest available version of each approved source when the brief is generated and shows the source timestamp or last-updated date. If a required source is unavailable or has been superseded, Juno labels the gap NEEDS CLARIFICATION and does not present affected findings as current. Operational signals use a default freshness threshold of 30 days. Approved strategy, roadmap, and decision documents remain valid until replaced, withdrawn, or formally expired.

## 02 Tools · System Capabilities

**The verb list**

READ — AUTO: Retrieve approved source content and metadata.
DRAFT — AUTO: Group related signals, compare them with strategy, roadmap, capacity, and commercial context, and generate an evidence-linked one-page brief. Flag assumptions, conflicting information, risks, open questions, and decisions requiring PM attention.
WRITE — CONFIRM: Save the approved final brief or update an approved team record only after explicit Product Manager confirmation.
SEND — BLOCKED: Juno cannot send messages, publish content, contact customers, or share the brief independently.

**Deliberate omissions**

Juno cannot autonomously edit Jira, update the roadmap, assign work, contact customers, send messages, or change source records. These actions are excluded because they create commitments or alter information that other people rely on. Juno is limited to retrieving approved sources, analyzing signals, drafting the brief, and saving an approved output only after Product Manager confirmation.

## 03 Loop · AI Costs & Latency

**Turn ceiling and escalation**

Juno first creates a draft by grouping and summarizing the available signals. It then checks every finding against the original sources for citation accuracy, conflicting evidence, and unsupported assumptions. If important information is still missing, Juno stops and marks it NEEDS CLARIFICATION rather than guessing.

**Latency and cost target**

Juno completes the draft and source-verification pass within 60 seconds under normal conditions. The task is limited to two model passes and one retrieval attempt per approved source. If a source remains unavailable, Juno stops waiting, records the gap, and completes the brief with a NEEDS CLARIFICATION label.

## 04 Memory · Data Requirements

**What persists, at what scope**

The approved brief, cited source references, PM-reviewed findings, and resolved clarification notes may persist at product-team level. Raw working notes, unverified assumptions, and sensitive source content do not become long-term memory automatically. Persisted information is available only to the Product Manager and product team that approved it and is not automatically shared across other teams or users.

**Expiry and write rules**

Approved briefs and PM-reviewed findings persist for 90 days, then require review before reuse. Only the Product Manager may approve information for memory or correct stored content. Human corrections always replace conflicting model-generated findings, while the original source references and correction history remain visible.

## 05 Permissions · AI Risks & Mitigations

**The permission tier**

| Side-effect class | Tier |
|---|---|
| read | auto |
| draft | auto |
| write | confirm |
| send | blocked |

**Justification**

Juno may read approved sources and draft the brief automatically because these actions do not change source systems or create commitments. Juno must ask for confirmation before saving the final brief or updating an approved team record because other people may rely on that information. Sending messages, publishing content, contacting customers, making product commitments, or communicating externally is blocked.

## 06 Verification · AI Testing & Measurement

**The check before output ships**

Every factual finding must link to an existing source. Juno checks that each source actually supports the finding and clearly distinguishes between single-source signals, findings supported by multiple independent sources, and reliable product data. Unsupported conclusions remain labelled as assumptions, and missing or conflicting information is marked NEEDS CLARIFICATION and escalated to the Product Manager.

**Failure behaviour**

If verification fails, Juno does not present the affected finding as confirmed. Juno returns the rest of the brief, labels the issue NEEDS CLARIFICATION, identifies the missing or conflicting source, and asks the Product Manager to review it. Juno never removes the issue silently or invents evidence to complete the brief.
